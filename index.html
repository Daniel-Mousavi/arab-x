About Arab-X

The national convening of Arab American enterprise.

Arab-X is a working assembly of the founders, corporations, investors, and public officials whose decisions determine where capital and contracts go.

Most conferences are organized around visibility. Arab-X is organized around transaction and consequence: a single-track program, a deliberately composed room, and partnership positions structured against every setting of the two days.

How It Is Built

Three design principles govern the program.

Concentration

The agenda stays focused on where Arab American enterprise has national leverage: business data, entrepreneurship pipelines, and MENA trade, rather than spreading thin across unrelated topics.

Alignment

Corporate partners, foundations, elected officials, universities, and diaspora leaders leave carrying the same narrative. Repetition across credible voices is what hardens a market position.

Concentrated attention

The program is single track, so the whole room hears the same conversation and leaves with the same reference points. The measure of the conference is what gets built afterward.

Conference Pillars

What the program is built on.

Arab-X programming is drawn from the four areas the Chamber is building nationally. Every panel on the agenda sits under one of them. Panel titles and speakers will be announced ahead of the conference.

National economic intelligence

Economic impact research, a national business index, verified directory work, and quarterly economic briefs. Power follows data, and the Chamber intends to own the numbers.

Capital and procurement infrastructure

Investment networks, corporate and municipal procurement pipelines, supplier certification, and long-term capital-access vehicles now in development.

National convening infrastructure

Arab-X, CEO roundtables, investor forums, policy convenings, and trade delegations, built as recurring national infrastructure rather than a calendar of events.

Institutional mediation and global trade

The Chamber as a bridge between MENA capital and U.S. markets, and as a credible intermediary between government and the business community.

Why Partner

Four returns. Structural, not incidental.

Sponsorship of Arab-X is a market-access decision.

Market access

Arab American consumers and businesses represent a growing, brand-loyal, and under-served commercial market. Arab-X puts your organization in front of its ownership class directly, in a room where credibility transfers.

Procurement and supplier pipeline

If your organization carries diverse-spend or local-sourcing targets, this is a qualified-vendor pipeline in one room, with the Chamber available to support verification, readiness, and follow-through.

Data and thought leadership

The Chamber is building the national economic intelligence layer for Arab American business. Partners are positioned alongside that work: on the panels, in the publications, and in the coverage that follows.

National visibility with substance

Founding partners of the inaugural Arab-X are named in the record of the first year of a convening built to run for decades, and are credited across the Chamber's channels well beyond the conference dates.
